About us

Queen of Sheba began in the 1990s as a business introducing Ethiopian products to the US market, and opened its Ethiopian restaurant in Portland in 1996.

Thanks to local press reviews and word of mouth, the restaurant became a Portland favorite and introduced many guests to Ethiopian spices, teas and chai.
